ENTITY DEFINITIONS

PER:
A person entity refers to a named individual human being.
Examples may include a person's full name, first name,
surname, or other identifying personal name.

ORG:
An organization entity refers to a named organization,
company, institution, political organization, team,
government body, or similar organized entity.

LOC:
A location entity refers to a named geographical place,
such as a city, country, state, district, village,
region, landmark, or other geographical location.

CARDINAL:
A cardinal entity refers to a numerical expression
annotated as CARDINAL in the TeluguNER dataset.

TIME:
A time entity refers to a temporal expression annotated
as TIME in the TeluguNER dataset, including dates, years,
days, months, clock times, durations, or other temporal
expressions.

OTH:
An OTH entity refers to an entity annotated as OTH in
the TeluguNER dataset.

ORGANISM:
An ORGANISM entity refers to an entity annotated as
ORGANISM in the TeluguNER dataset.
